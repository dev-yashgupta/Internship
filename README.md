## Week 1 Project

**Title:** Predicting Repeat Purchases in Online Skincare Retail

**Domain:** Beauty & Wellness / E-commerce Analytics

**Objective:** Define a business problem and propose a data science workflow to identify customers likely to make repeat purchases. The proposed work covers customer behaviour analysis, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, classification modelling, model evaluation, and retention-oriented business recommendations.

The Week 1 deliverable is a research and project proposal. It does not claim that a predictive model has already been trained or evaluated.

See [Week 1 project documentation](Week-01-Beauty-Wellness/README.md).
