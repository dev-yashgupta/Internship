## Week 1 Project

**Title:** Predicting Repeat Purchases in Online Skincare Retail

**Domain:** Beauty & Wellness / E-commerce Analytics

**Objective:** Define a business problem and propose a data science workflow to identify customers likely to make repeat purchases. The proposed work covers customer behaviour analysis, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, classification modelling, model evaluation, and retention-oriented business recommendations.

The Week 1 deliverable is a research and project proposal. Dataset validation, exploratory analysis, model training, and empirical evaluation are future steps; no model results are claimed at this stage.

See [Week 1 project documentation](Week-01-Beauty-Wellness/README.md).

## Project Workflow

1. Review the business problem and verify the dataset source and licence.
2. Define the observation and prediction windows.
3. Audit and explore the data.
4. Engineer leakage-safe customer features.
5. Train a baseline and compare candidate classifiers.
6. Evaluate on a held-out test set and document business implications.

## Data and Privacy

Only use datasets that are public, properly licensed, or otherwise authorized for the project. Do not commit personal customer information, credentials, API keys, or confidential internship data. Local raw datasets are excluded by the repository's `.gitignore`.
